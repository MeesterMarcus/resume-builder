Product roadmap

Building the useful parts.
Keeping the friction out.

RapidCV is growing from a focused local résumé builder into a more capable job-search companion—without losing the speed, clarity, and privacy that make it useful today.

Direction, not a deadline

This roadmap shows current thinking, not guaranteed release dates. Priorities may change as the product learns from real users.

What comes next

A practical path forward.

Each stage builds on the same principle: help people present their experience clearly while keeping them in control of their data and final decisions.

Optional accounts + managed AI

Registration without lock-in

Accounts should add convenience and safer managed AI access, not become a requirement. The local-first builder would remain useful without signing in.

  • Email or passkey sign-in
  • Cross-device résumé sync
  • Cloud backup and restore
  • Metered managed AI so most users never handle a personal API key
  • Clear usage limits and account controls
  • Account data export and deletion
  • Clear local-versus-cloud controls

Product principles

What will not change.

01

Optional accounts

Signing in should unlock sync and convenience—not basic access.

02

AI stays reviewable

Suggestions should be clear, reversible, and grounded in what the user actually provided.

03

Portable by default

Users should be able to export their résumé and leave with their work.

04

Small and focused

New features must earn their place without making the core builder slower or harder to use.
